## Caveat types

- **FLOOR_EFFECT**: the focal method reaches its targets at the shortest run time tested on most hosts, so its margin is censored: the ladder cannot show how much faster it is
- **SATURATION**: every strong method ends at the same value on (almost) every host, so the instances can only separate methods by speed, never by solution quality
- **UNCERTIFIED_TARGETS**: many targets are best-known values rather than certified optima, so "reached the target" may not mean "solved"
- **UNRESOLVED_VERDICT**: a preregistered comparison ended UNRESOLVED or MIXED: the study lacked the power or the resolution to decide it
- **COMPONENT_EFFECT_UNRESOLVED**: removing a component of the method had no measurable (or no resolved) effect, so whether it matters is unknown
- **INVALID_RUN**: the study failed its own validity check; nothing it measured can be read
- **INTERRUPTED**: the run was interrupted and resumed; the resume must be disclosed and the design should expect it
- **MISSING_BASELINE**: a stronger published solver for this problem class was not among the competitors
- **REGIME_GAP**: the study's longest budget is below the competitors' home regime (the budgets they were tuned for)
- **SIZE_CAP**: the instances sit at the task model's maximum size, so the study cannot say what happens beyond it
- **ARM_NOT_RESPONDING**: a competitor ends at about the same value at every run time on most hosts, well short of the target: either the budgets are outside its regime or its harness is wrong, and the comparison with it means nothing until one of the two is known
- **OUTPERFORMED**: a competitor reaches the targets more than twice as fast as the system's own method on a quarter of the hosts or more: the method has a weakness there (or, if the competitor is one of the system's earlier methods, a regression)
- **STALE_KNOWLEDGE**: the cell's own knowledge of the field (its baseline catalog) predates the newest Evaluation, so MISSING_BASELINE cannot be trusted until the catalog is refreshed
