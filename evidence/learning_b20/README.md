# B20 evidence: learning to research (W18–W20, E3), DEV only

Builder-generated. Nothing here was reviewed independently. There is no sealed panel or
custodian, so the better-improver question (E3, GV4) stays **explicitly UNESTABLISHED**.
Every result below is a DEV observation.

## Domain

Equational research in Boolean algebra (`src/genesis/learning/`), axiomatized three
ways:
- Sheffer's 3-axiom basis and Wolfram's single axiom, both in the Sheffer stroke;
- Huntington's basis in + and n.

Truth is decided exactly: the variety is generated by the 2-element algebra. A lemma
enters a library only with an E proof from the axioms plus earlier lemmas. Successor
proofs that use lemmas are replayed in Lean with their whole lemma chain
(`fof_lean.replay(lemmas=…, goal_deps=…, goal_positions=…)`).

**A run.** A research run spends a CPU budget choosing conjectures from a pool, checking
them with the decider, and proving the true ones. Its successor is the frozen library.
The successor is evaluated on unused targets, with 1 s per target (3 s on Wolfram) and
the 8 most relevant lemmas as premises.

## Main run (`b20_learning.py`; `run.log`, 2,457 s)

**Calibration** (`calibration.json`). The rules were fixed in the script.
- Sheffer: target range 5–8 nodes, research budget 1.57 s.
- Huntington: no range fell in the [0.2, 0.6] band, so the rule chose the range closest
  to 0.4 (6–9 nodes); research budget 1.0 s.
- The empty-library rates come from E with CPU limits on a loaded machine, so a re-run
  can choose differently.

**Learned policy** (`train/policy.json`). Logistic weights from 646 examples drawn from
18 training runs (3 seeds × 2 systems × 3 policies). The weights were frozen before any
evaluation run; their SHA-256 is recorded. Training Brier score 0.23, against 0.25 for
no information.

**W19 / E3** (`w19/rows.json`, `e3/summary.json`). Five policies, 2 systems × 4 fresh
seeds = 8 independent research runs per policy. Mean targets proved out of 25:

| empty | fixed | random | heuristic | bandit | learned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18.1 | 19.75 | 17.9 | 19.9 | 19.9 | 19.9 |

- **Learned vs fixed.** 1 win, 7 ties, 0 losses (+0.125). Against the best baseline it
  scores −0.125.
- **Calibration.** On evaluation runs the learned policy's Brier score is 0.23–0.26:
  close to uninformative.
- **Diversity.** Bandit and learned explore every conjecture class. Fixed and heuristic
  try 5–8 of 9–11.
- **Ceiling.** Huntington successors sit at the ceiling (24–25 of 25), so most ties are
  ceiling ties.
- **Interference.** On Huntington seed 204, a random run that proved a single lemma
  scored 11/25, below the empty library's 20/25. A small, irrelevant library can make E
  worse.

**W18 macros** (`w18/`). A macro is the dependency closure of the lemmas a successor
actually used, in order, with per-step budgets.
- **Faithful replay.** Expanding a macro on its source system proves every step again,
  in both systems.
- **Compression.** Description length shrinks from 4,046 to 1,864 characters (Sheffer)
  and from 4,870 to 1,164 (Huntington).
- **Same system, unseen seeds.** With the induction cost charged, macro minus plain
  research is [0, 0, +1, 0, 0, −1, 0, 0].
- **Revocation.** Revoking the first step invalidates every later step: the macros are
  single dependency chains.
- **Transfer to Wolfram's axiom.** The strict macro failed at its first step, which is
  commutativity: 10 s was not enough from that axiom. Every later step was therefore
  skipped, and the macro scored 0 of 15. Plain research with the same 240 s budget reached
  6 of 15; the empty library reaches 0.

**W20 memory and curriculum** (`w20/rows.json`).
- Memory across seeds is screened against the evaluation targets (2 INSTANCE exclusions),
  then retrieved, replayed whole, or extended with a curriculum built from development
  targets.
- All arms are within one target of each other: no effect where there is no headroom.
- Demotion shrank memory as designed.

**Lean** (`lean/replays.json`). Of the 68 successor proofs that used lemmas (583 chained
lemmas in all), 67 replay to THEOREM_CHECKED. The remaining one hit the sandbox resource
limit.

## Follow-ups on Wolfram's single axiom (written after the main run)

E proves none of the evaluation targets from Wolfram's axiom alone, so this is the one
system where research can matter.

1. **`e3_hard/`, `b20_e3_hard.py`.** The five policies on fresh seeds 311–314, with the
   main run's budget rule (a quarter of the fixed policy's pool-exhaustion CPU, which
   gives 4.23 s) and 10 s per proof attempt.
   - The budget was not enough: almost every run spent it on the first, hard conjecture
     and proved nothing.
   - The exception is one random run (seed 313), which proved 2 lemmas and then 7 of 15
     targets.
   - A first attempt with 240 s was stopped because the pool was exhausted, so the
     policies could not differ. Its log is kept.
2. **`hard_followup/`** (`b20_hard_followup.py`).
   - **Non-strict macro expansion.** Added after the strict transfer failed: each step is
     attempted with whatever earlier steps were proved. It proved all 46 steps from
     Wolfram's axiom and reached 6 of 15. That equals plain research at the same budget;
     the strict macro reached 0.
   - **Curriculum.** Built from the failed development targets (never the evaluation
     targets), its 60 s gave 12, 11, 0 and 10 of 15 on the four seeds. The memory arms
     had nothing to work with, because the fixed libraries of `e3_hard/` were empty.
3. **`curriculum_control.json`** (`b20_curriculum_control.py`). The effort-matched control:
   plain research and curriculum each get 60 CPU-s on the same seed, and every library is
   screened against the evaluation targets before use.

   | Seed | Empty library | Plain, 60 s | Curriculum, 60 s | Both |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 311 | 0 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
   | 312 | 0 | 9 | 11 | 10 |
   | 313 | 0 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
   | 314 | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 |

   - **Research.** On this system, research is what matters: 60 s of it raises the
     successor from 0 to 8–12 of 15.
   - **Curriculum.** It matches or beats plain research: +2 on one seed, a tie on three.
     That is no clear curriculum-specific gain.
   - **Screening.** It removed 1–4 INSTANCE lemmas per library.

**Concurrency caveat.** The hard-system runs shared two cores with the B19 rehearsal and
with each other. E's results near its time limits then vary between runs. On seed 313,
the follow-up's curriculum solved 1 variant where the control's solved 34. Single runs
on this system are therefore noisy.

## What this does not show

- A better improver: not established, and not testable without sealed panels.
- That the learned policy is better than fixed rules.
- Macro transfer across systems.
- That this domain needs research at all, except from Wolfram's axiom.
