 1. Executive definition and design freeze
 Genesis is a proof-gated, causally self-studying, representation-inventing, tool-building and
 development-designing discovery system. Its purpose is not to maximize the apparent intelligence of a single
 language-model session. Its purpose is to increase the set of problems for which a system can produce
 independently checkable results, while learning reusable mechanisms that make future verified discovery
 cheaper and more reliable.
 The central object is a research program expressed in typed Research IR. Generative models, symbolic
 solvers, theorem provers, simulators, numerical methods and human input are untrusted proposal sources.
 They may suggest candidate artifacts, but only protected checkers and prospective evaluation can promote
 those artifacts into trusted evidence.

 Genesis is deliberately split into three strata. The Evidence Microkernel is small and externally governed. The
 Cognitive Kernel contains the provisional minimal instruction set used to construct research programs. The
 Generated Phenotype contains almost everything domain-specific: temporary agents, specialist solvers,
 memories, representations, curricula, experiments and compiled operators. The design goal is to make the
 core smaller while the closure of what it can generate becomes larger.

 The design is now frozen. New named engines are treated as macros unless a measured failure demonstrates
 that the existing primitive basis cannot express the needed behavior within acceptable cost. This prevents the
 architecture from becoming a catalogue of overlapping metaphors.




           Figure 1. Final three-stratum architecture. The trusted root is intentionally smaller than the evolvable system.


 1.1 One-sentence principle
 Creative generator -> typed executable proposal -> bounded execution -> independent
 checker/proof replay -> immutable evidence -> fresh prospective evaluation -> retain, reject,
 compress, or branch.

 1.2 The non-negotiable scientific claim boundary
 Genesis may generate ambitious conjectures, architectures and self-modifications. None of these count as
 improvement because a model says they are good, because a source file exists, because a unit test passes, or
 because a retrospective benchmark improves after tuning. A claim is released only at the strongest evidence
 level actually supported by execution records. Unsupported, broken, rejected and unsettled outcomes remain
 distinct.

 The deepest target claim is not “the system can modify itself.” The target is stronger and harder: a successor
 should become measurably better at producing future independently verified improvements on tasks that
 were not available when that successor was constructed. That requires multi-generation, prospective,
 leak-free evaluation.

 1.3 What Genesis is not
   • Not a claim that open mathematical problems will be solved automatically.
   • Not a single monolithic model. Models are replaceable proposal sources and specialist components.
   • Not an unrestricted autonomous actor. Capability growth and authority growth are separated.
   • Not a self-certifying loop. Candidate verifiers, objectives and evaluators cannot promote themselves.
   • Not an architecture that assumes one representation, one proof system, one model family, or one research
      strategy is universally best.
   • Not an excuse to treat synthetic benchmarks as evidence of mathematical discovery.

 2. Formal model
 2.1 System version
 A system version is an immutable identity over all components that can influence a comparison:
   SystemVersion = (
     solver, tool_library, memory_snapshot, curriculum_policy,
     research_policy, model_configuration, cognitive_kernel,
     verifier_set, resolved_config, dependency_lock, environment_digest
   )

 A version identifier is content-derived. A comparison is invalid if the actual execution environment differs
 materially from the registered version without an explicit deviation record.

 2.2 Research state
 Let the mutable research state at time t be:
   X_t = (B_t, H_t, R_t, C_t, E_t, W_t, L_t, A_t)

   H_t : hypothesis and theory population

 Actions are typed interventions u_t. The system learns a causal dynamics model X_{t+1} = f(X_t, u_t,
 epsilon_t), but this model is advisory: it predicts interventions; it does not certify outcomes.

 2.3 Capability, reachability and improver quality
 Current performance is insufficient as the sole target. Genesis tracks at least three conceptually different
 quantities: verified capability V(S), future verified reachability R_H(S), and improvement productivity A(S).
   V(S)   = verified capability on current/fresh tasks
   R_H(S) = set/value of useful states reachable within horizon H
   A(S)   = new independently verified capabilities acquired per unit cost/time

 A multi-generation better-improver claim requires evidence that the process creating successors improves,
 not merely that one descendant scores better. The project should prefer Pareto frontiers over a single grand
 scalar. Any scalar objective is a convenience for local scheduling, not the final truth standard.

 2.4 Authority invariant
   Capability_{t+1} > Capability_t       is allowed and desired.
   Authority_{t+1} <= Authority_t          unless an external controller explicitly changes it.

 Forking, branching or creating many descendants must not duplicate permissions, budgets, credentials, or
 release authority. External resources are leased by the microkernel and accounted to a parent campaign.

 2.5 Research as typed program synthesis
 A research program is a directed acyclic or cyclicly-controlled graph of typed operators. The target is not
 “generate a good answer” but “construct an artifact of a required evidence type.” Planning can therefore
 work backward from the target type, much like type inhabitation or program synthesis.

 3.2 Trust root and reflective limits
 Genesis must never justify unlimited trust by circular self-assertion. A trust-dependency graph records which
 verifier checked which artifact and which assumptions/verifier implementation each result depends on. Cycles

 promotion.

 The root may be upgraded, but a root upgrade is a new release operation requiring external authorization and
 independent replay. A candidate verifier may be generated and adversarially tested by Genesis; it cannot
 make itself authoritative.

 3.3 Why a small kernel matters
 The broader the mutable architecture becomes, the more important it is that correctness depend on a small,
 boring substrate. This follows the same general principle as proof-carrying code: complex or untrusted
 producers may supply artifacts, while a smaller trusted checker enforces the contract [R6]. Lean is attractive
 for formal mathematical evidence because its small kernel checks proof terms independently of the tactics or
 models that produced them [R5].

 5. The complete discovery loop




                                    Figure 4. End-to-end loop from goal to meta-gap discovery.


 5.1 Stage A - contract and faithful formalization
 Before solving, Genesis converts the request into a typed contract: inputs, assumptions, guarantee, verifier,
 budget and authority. Ambiguity is represented rather than silently guessed. If two plausible formalizations
 imply materially different outputs, the ambiguity becomes an obligation to resolve or an explicit branch.

 Formalization correctness is a separate problem from proof correctness. A proof assistant can certify a
 theorem that faithfully formalizes the wrong human statement. Therefore the formalization compiler retains
 natural-language anchors, units, domain constraints, quantifier mappings and counterexample tests that
 attempt to falsify the intended equivalence.

 5.2 Stage B - identifiability and ignorance analysis
 Given evidence E, define the space of admissible worlds W(E). If two admissible worlds produce different
 target values, the answer is not identified. Genesis returns UNIDENTIFIED, not a fabricated precise conclusion.
 It then searches for the cheapest experiment, measurement, assumption or capability that separates the
 worlds.
 Unknown states are classified: DATA_LIMITED, MEASUREMENT_LIMITED, MODEL_AMBIGUOUS,
 CAPABILITY_LIMITED, COMPUTE_LIMITED, NONIDENTIFIABLE, FORMALLY_INDEPENDENT, STOCHASTIC or
 CHECKER_LIMITED. Each diagnosis maps to a different response policy.

 5.3 Stage C - gap diagnosis before brute force
 The Gap Engine treats failure as a diagnosis problem. It asks which element of the current search process is
 limiting: representation, missing concept, missing theorem, missing capability, verifier weakness, experiment
 design, insufficient resource, unidentifiability, or a search class whose performance is already near a certified
 barrier.

 The central operational rule is: when progress stalls, change what is being searched before merely increasing
 the amount of search.

GENESIS - Final Architecture & Implementation Specification                                             5. The complete discovery loop




 5.4 Stage D - competing mathematical/scientific worlds
 Maintain a bounded ecology of incompatible hypotheses and representations. Worlds are born, tested,
 hybridized and retired under explicit limits. The default reference policy is max 8 active worlds, <= 4 births
 per epoch, <= 1 hybrid birth per epoch, two development panels per world unless invalid, lineage depth <=
 8, <= 4 root actions per world per epoch and <= 8 shared experiments per epoch. These are starting
 engineering limits, not scientific constants.

 5.5 Stage E - construction and obstruction in parallel




                              Figure 5. Construction and obstruction meet at a certified bound state.

 For optimization or extremal mathematics, every campaign should attempt both constructive lower bounds
 and obstructive upper bounds. A Ghost is an independently checked structure that satisfies the visible axioms
 while falsifying the goal. A validated ghost is evidence that the proposed theorem/assumption set is
 insufficient; failure to find a ghost is not proof.

 5.6 Stage F - certificate-first obligation graph
 Large goals are decomposed into obligations whose leaf nodes have cheap, explicit checkers: Lean kernel,
 SAT/PB/MaxSAT certificate, exact computation, property test, interval enclosure, or hash/replay check.
 Delegation is allowed only when child guarantees compose into the parent guarantee. Budgets and
 permissions attenuate downward; delegation cannot expand authority.

 5.7 Stage G - prospective evaluation and retention
 Useful-looking artifacts remain DEV_ONLY until a frozen candidate is evaluated on fresh confirmation data.
 Confirmation data cannot tune the candidate, choose among candidates for the same claim, or repair the
 candidate after inspection. Every attempt and manual intervention is charged. The judge recomputes
 outcomes from execution evidence rather than trusting worker summaries.

 8.1 c4_host_v2

   • Same general host-graph interface with n <= 63.

 10. Local-to-global, continuation and tractability
 10.1 Learned covers and gluing
 Genesis may cover a large problem X by overlapping regions U_i, solve each region in its best representation,
 translate onto overlaps and attempt to glue the local solutions. Pairwise and higher-order inconsistencies
 become explicit obstruction artifacts rather than being averaged away. The cover itself is searched to balance
 local solvability against interface cost.

 10.2 Continuation
 When a target is distant from known solved instances, synthesize a path P(lambda), lambda in [0,1], from an
 easy problem to the target. Carry solution structure and certificates along the path using predictor/corrector
 updates. Bifurcation, hysteresis or path failure becomes a diagnostic signal for missing capability or regime
 change. Certified numerical homotopy tracking demonstrates that path-following can be combined with
 rigorous guarantees in narrower numerical settings [R8].

 10.3 Parameterized tractability and kernels
 Search for hidden parameters k(P) such that the apparent global complexity is governed by a small effective
 parameter. If a sound reduction produces a kernel K(P) whose size is bounded by f(k), solve K exactly and lift
 the certificate. The system must distinguish empirically predictive parameters from formally established
 fixed-parameter tractability; it may not label the latter without proof.

 10.4 Approximation hierarchy
 Cheap approximations are allowed to guide search if their epistemic type remains approximate. A preferred
 pattern is approximate proposal -> certified neighborhood/bound -> exact or formal completion. This avoids
 both extremes: expensive exact search everywhere and unprincipled approximate answers at the trust
 boundary.


 11. Fault-tolerant cognition
 Genesis assumes that models, solvers, heuristics and even checkers can fail. Global reliability should not
 require every component to be nearly perfect.

 11.1 Heterogeneous redundancy
 For critical claims, construct structurally different witnesses: formal proof, independent exact computation,
 alternate derivation, adversarial counterexample search, or second checker implementation. Agreement from
 components that share the same failure source is discounted by dependency-aware evidence fusion.

 11.2 Semantic parity checks
 Reasoning DAGs contain redundant implications and invariants that act like parity constraints. When a check
 fails, the pattern of violated constraints is used as a syndrome to localize the earliest likely corrupted region.
 Only the affected subtree is resynthesized, then downstream artifacts are replayed.

 11.3 Adaptive verification strength
 Use weak inexpensive checks broadly and reserve expensive strong verification for high-criticality or
 uncertain nodes. Criticality is a function of downstream dependence, impact if false, irreversibility and
 correlated-failure risk.

 12.3 Self-science protocol
 Each retained improvement should ideally move through hypothesis -> intervention -> prediction -> matched
 execution -> causal analysis -> prospective confirmation -> mechanism crystallization. Correlated gains
 remain labeled CORRELATED. Ablation upgrades the claim to ABLATION_SUPPORTED; placebo and
 discriminating controls can upgrade to PLACEBO_CONTROLLED.

 12.4 Better-improver test
 To claim that a successor is a better improver, compare two frozen research policies on the same future
 improvement opportunities. Measure not only the quality of the immediate solver but the quality, transfer and
 cost of the improvements each policy creates. This is a meta-level prospective experiment, not an
 extrapolation from one successful self-edit.

 13. Experience synthesis and developmental
 programs
 Genesis treats training data and curricula as causal interventions on capability formation. A developmental
 program maps student state to the next experience, tool exposure, representation challenge or feedback
 regime. The objective is durable, transferable capability and future learnability, not short-term training loss.

 13.1 Minimal teaching sets
 Search for small sets of experiences that reliably induce a target mechanism. Paired counterfactual examples
 should break shortcuts while preserving the intended latent structure. The causal chain experience ->
 mechanism -> capability should be tested rather than assumed.

 13.2 Critical periods and ordering
 Learning order matters. Genesis estimates which prerequisites must exist before an experience becomes
 productive and whether some mechanisms are easier to install at particular stages. This creates a
 developmental partial order rather than a shuffled curriculum.

 13.3 Teach, unteach, repair
 When a shortcut or harmful mechanism is identified, the controller may remove its source data, introduce
 counterexamples, isolate the mechanism, or train an antidataset designed to suppress it while preserving
 useful capabilities. These are candidate interventions subject to sealed regression testing.


 14. Cognitive crystallization and compilation
 Repeatedly useful intelligence should not remain trapped inside expensive opaque inference if it can be
 extracted into a smaller verified form.

 14.1 Compilation ladder
   reasoning trace
     -> causal memory
     -> reusable procedure
     -> typed operator
     -> specialized small model
     -> symbolic program / solver rule
     -> optimized native kernel / cache / index

 Promotion is evidence-gated. A compiled artifact must preserve designated behavior over fresh tasks, reduce
 cost or increase reliability, and expose rollback. If a symbolic replacement cannot faithfully capture the
 learned capability, a hybrid or neural representation remains valid.

 14.2 Law ladder
   CORRELATION -> PREDICTIVE_RULE -> CAUSAL_MECHANISM -> SYMBOLIC_LAW -> DERIVED_THEOREM

 The ladder separates interpretability from proof. A compact equation fitted to neural behavior is not a
 theorem. Each promotion requires stronger interventions, invariance tests, derivation or formal checking.

 14.3 Generative kernel objective
 Let K be the candidate primitive basis and M_i the macro capabilities. Search for a compact K plus short
 compiler programs C_i such that C_i(K) reconstructs all required macros. Remove a primitive if it can be
 efficiently synthesized from the remainder. Retain a derivable primitive when it offers substantial
 computational advantage as a compiled instruction.


 15. Reflective trust, foundations and formal limits
 Genesis must be capable of concluding that its current formal system or verifier hierarchy is insufficient. This
 is not failure; it is a typed result that triggers foundation lifting or external meta-level checking.




                                                              24
GENESIS - Final Architecture & Implementation Specification                       15. Reflective trust, foundations and formal limits




 15.1 Trust tower
 Object-level systems produce candidate proofs and modifications. Meta-level checkers reason about the
 object-level artifacts. The system records trust height and exact verifier identity. Claims do not silently inherit
 new trust because a verifier version changed.

 15.2 Foundation lifting
 When T cannot settle a target, Genesis may search for a minimal extension T + A, a different formal
 foundation, or a checked translation into another theory. It should prefer conservative definitional extensions
 where possible. Independence candidates require models or metatheory strong enough to establish both
 sides, not merely repeated proof-search failure.

 15.3 No self-certification escape hatch
 There is intentionally no “ultimate internal verifier.” The system can improve how it constructs and checks
 evidence, but the accepted root of trust remains externally governed. This is a design invariant, not a
 temporary limitation.

 18.1 Split classes
 Use physically and logically separate DEV, SELECT, CONFIRM and SEALED/EVALUATOR assets. Research
 workers can use DEV. SELECT may be used only according to the preregistered selection protocol.
 CONFIRM/SEALED data must not influence candidate construction for the claim being evaluated.

 18.2 Leak-screening pipeline
   • Exact content hashes across all visible and sealed statements.
   • Canonicalized statement hashes after alpha-renaming, whitespace normalization and benign syntactic

      representation-independent fingerprints.

     evaluator ledger.

     sealed targets.
   • After freeze, a discovered leak invalidates the affected claim; the correct action is rerun after repair, not
      statistical adjustment.

 19. Clean experimental program
 19.1 Experiment A - microkernel and replay
 Goal: establish L0. Every execution is reproducible from version/config/artifact hashes; checker outputs can
 be replayed; intentional corruption is detected; evaluator files remain inaccessible to research workers;
 resource receipts reconcile with actual process limits. No intelligence claim is made.

 19.2 Experiment B - Ghost Census kill gate
 Use a preregistered formal problem family in which countermodels are meaningful and exact. Compare
 ghost-driven lemma/assumption selection against the strongest conventional baseline available under
 matched budgets and randomized order. States are PROVED, GHOST_FOUND, UNSETTLED and
 HARNESS_ERROR. A finite failure to find a model is never reinterpreted as consistency proof.

 19.3 Experiment C - clean prospective Lean study
 Target: a substantial sealed panel on the order of ~700 theorems that the evaluated system has not seen,
 stratified by theorem family/difficulty and screened against all research-visible material. Freeze code,
 memory, prompts, tool versions, model identifiers, theorem library revision and evaluator before exposure.
 The exact panel construction should be finalized by the independent evaluator and preregistered, not tuned
 to achieve significance.
 Primary comparison: base system versus Genesis research policy under a fixed resource envelope. Primary
 metric should be paired exact theorem success or another preregistered binary/ordinal outcome with a test
 appropriate to clustering by theorem family. Secondary metrics include proof-check success, total cost, wall
 time, retries, calibration, new tool usage, failure taxonomy and sensitivity to project clustering. Report every
 exclusion and deviation.

GENESIS - Final Architecture & Implementation Specification                                             19. Clean experimental program




 19.4 Experiment D - transfer
 Take retained mechanisms from one domain and evaluate them on a frozen new theorem family or the
 triangle-free host domain. Transfer means the mechanism was not selected using the target confirmation
 panel. Merely re-running the same search code on a new input is not sufficient if all hyperparameters/policies
 were tuned after inspecting the target.

 19.5 Experiment E - better improver
 Create two research systems differing in improvement policy. Give both the same sequence of future
 improvement opportunities, budgets and permissible evidence. Measure the quality/cost/transfer of the
 modifications they produce. A better-improver claim requires the successor policy to outperform on the
 production of future improvements, not just on the tasks used to create it.

 19.6 Experiment F - multi-generation
 Require at least three clean generations with fresh confirmation material per generation and a preregistered
 trend analysis. Freeze and archive every ancestor. A generation that fails does not disappear from the cost
 ledger. If the trend collapses after compute matching or independent replication, the recursive-improvement
 claim is rejected or narrowed.

 24. Current status and what remains unproven
 The architecture is much further developed than its scientific evidence. The synthetic Gap Engine
 experiments are useful evidence about interaction-search mechanisms, not about mathematical
 self-improvement. At small candidate counts, random-subset interaction discovery performed strongly; at
 larger counts it collapsed until likely-partner activation was introduced. That supports the design decision not
 to kill individually weak ideas too early, but it does not prove that the same mechanism discovers useful
 mathematical research procedures.




                                                                           33
GENESIS - Final Architecture & Implementation Specification                               24. Current status and what remains unproven



 The recent Lean round cannot be used as clean confirmation because of sealed-set contamination. Therefore
 the decisive near-term work is not another conceptual engine. It is the clean prospective experiment plus a
 fully replayable evidence kernel.

 24.1 Explicitly unproven claims
   • That Genesis can repeatedly invent genuinely useful new mathematical concepts.
   • That learned research procedures improve the production of future improvements after compute
      matching.
   • That the system can sustain improvement over multiple clean generations.
   • That the minimal cognitive basis can be discovered rather than merely hand-designed.
   • That mechanism crystallization can routinely extract faithful symbolic laws from large learned models.
   • That automated foundation lifting or ontology invention will be broadly useful outside narrow domains.
   • That any observed gains generalize across mathematics, software and empirical science without
      domain-specific engineering.

 24.2 What would change the assessment
 The most important evidence would be a clean sequence in which Genesis itself diagnoses a capability gap
 not explicitly encoded by its designers, synthesizes a new operator or procedure, passes independent
 verification, improves performance on fresh sealed problems, transfers the mechanism to a new family, and
 then uses the retained mechanism to produce another useful improvement. That is substantially more
 informative than a large static architecture diagram.


 25. Design-freeze rules for future “pushes”
 After this specification, a new idea is not automatically a new permanent subsystem. It enters through the
 following novelty gate.

  Question                           Pass condition

  Observed need?                     A reproducible frontier failure exists; not merely conceptual elegance.

  Expressible already?               Existing cognitive primitives/macros cannot express an adequate solution within the registered cost
                                     bound.

  Distinct evidence?                 The proposed mechanism creates a prediction or capability not reducible to existing macros.

  Implementable?                     There is a typed interface, execution path, checker/evaluator and rollback.

  Prospective value?                 Frozen addition improves fresh tasks or reduces verified cost in a preregistered comparison.

  Ablation-essential?                Removing it removes the gain or imposes a clearly measured compilation penalty.

  Compressible later?                If it becomes redundant, the Generative Kernel may demote it from primitive to macro.


 This rule makes “improvement #61” an empirical discovery problem. The architecture is intentionally
 designed to become smaller in its core while increasing the closure of capabilities it can generate.

 28. Scientific literature and external knowledge
 compiler
 Retrieval alone is insufficient. Genesis should compile literature into typed claims: statement, assumptions,
 method, evidence class, dataset, replication status, dependencies, contradictory findings and priority date.
 External papers are evidence sources, not ground truth. Their claims retain their published scope and
 uncertainty.

 Novelty is orthogonal to correctness. A theorem may be correct but already known; an idea may be novel but
 wrong. Priority checking therefore occurs after internal validity checks and before public novelty claims.

 29. Domain expansion beyond mathematics
 29.1 Software research
 Use executable specifications, tests, formal contracts, fuzzing, static analysis and performance counters. The
 system can synthesize patches, but code that modifies protected evaluator/runner paths is rejected.
 Performance improvements require matched hardware/environment and regression coverage.

 29.2 Empirical science
 Add instrument models, calibration chains, missingness/selection bias, multi-fidelity simulation and real
 experimental interfaces. The evidence microkernel must preserve which conclusions are simulation-only
 versus measured. Physical-world execution should remain brokered and approval-gated.

 29.3 Machine-learning research
 Treat data, objectives, training recipes, model versions and hardware as first-class evidence dependencies.
 Synthetic self-training is not assumed to add information unless an external signal such as verifier feedback,
 environment interaction or new data actually constrains the learner.


 30. Security and operational boundary
 Genesis is a capability architecture, not an authority-escalation architecture. Generated code runs with no
 network by default. It may not acquire credentials, money, cloud accounts, persistent external infrastructure
 or hidden communication channels. External actions are performed only through registered brokers that
 enforce the campaign grant and produce receipts.

 If recursive cross-domain improvement ever becomes empirically strong, the correct response is not
 automatic scaling. Freeze the release, reproduce on independent infrastructure, audit the causal chain and
 failure modes, then deliberately decide whether to increase resources.

 33. Coordinator and campaign state machine
 The Coordinator is the only persistent writer to authoritative campaign state. Workers are ephemeral. The
 state machine below should be explicit in code so a crashed process cannot skip an evidence gate.
   CREATED
     -> VALIDATED_CONFIG
     -> DEV_RUNNING
     -> CANDIDATE_FROZEN
     -> LEAK_SCAN_PASSED
     -> SEALED_RUNNING
     -> JUDGED
     -> RELEASED | REJECTED | INVALIDATED

   Any state -> ABORTED on policy/resource/operator failure.
   Any post-freeze state -> INVALIDATED if contamination or unauthorized mutation is discovered.


 33.1 Transaction boundary
 A worker response is never authoritative on arrival. The Coordinator validates its schema, checks referenced
 artifacts exist and match hashes, validates the worker grant, stores immutable outputs, inserts
 attempt/resource records, then performs evidence transitions inside one SQLite transaction. If any step fails,
 the transaction rolls back while the raw untrusted work directory remains available for forensic inspection.

 33.2 Recovery after crash
   • On startup, verify the append-only event hash chain and reconcile every running attempt against
     OS/container state.
   • Attempts with no live process and no terminal receipt become INTERRUPTED, never silently SUCCESS.

   • Never resume a sealed run under a changed SystemVersion; create a new evaluation attempt with an
      explicit deviation.


 34. Research market and scheduling algorithm
 Scheduling is a value-of-computation problem. Each proposal declares expected immediate progress,
 information gain, future capability value, cost, verifier demand and irreversibility. The scheduler does not
 need a perfect utility model; it needs calibrated comparisons and explicit exploration budget.
   score(a) = E[verified_progress(a)]
            + lambda_info * E[information_gain(a)]
            + lambda_cap * E[future_capability_value(a)]
            + lambda_opt * option_value(a)
            - lambda_cost * expected_cost(a)
            - lambda_risk * irreversibility(a)

   Selection = Pareto prefilter + budgeted portfolio optimization + reserved diversity quota

 At least one slot in a nontrivial epoch should remain available for a structurally different hypothesis or
 research policy when the archive indicates premature convergence. The exact exploration quota is tunable on
 DEV, but it must be frozen before confirmation.

 34.1 Stop-thinking rule
 For an active obligation, estimate the best remaining value of computation. If every legal action has predicted
 marginal value below its resource price and there is no unresolved high-criticality verification obligation,
 terminate as UNSETTLED or return the current anytime-correct bound. “Keep thinking” is not the default.


 35. Leak scanner and contamination firewall
 Because the strongest current project failure was sealed-theorem leakage, the leak scanner is part of the
 scientific kernel rather than a convenience script.
   def screen(candidate_artifact, sealed_index):
       exact = sha256(canonical_bytes(candidate_artifact))
       if sealed_index.exact.contains(exact): return BLOCK(EXACT_MATCH)

        ast = parse_and_alpha_normalize(candidate_artifact)
        structural = structural_fingerprint(ast)
        if sealed_index.structural.contains(structural): return BLOCK(STRUCTURAL_MATCH)

        features = near_duplicate_features(ast)
        neighbors = sealed_index.ann.query(features, k=K)
        if max_similarity(neighbors) >= HARD_THRESHOLD: return BLOCK(NEAR_DUPLICATE)
        if max_similarity(neighbors) >= REVIEW_THRESHOLD: return QUARANTINE_FOR_ADJUDICATION
        return ALLOW

 The scanner is applied not only to initial datasets but also to generated lemmas, memorized examples,
 procedure libraries, retrieved documents and automatically synthesized curricula before those artifacts
 become visible to research workers. Screening decisions and thresholds are part of the evaluator version.

 36.2 Worked C4 research trace
 A typical c4_host_v2 campaign begins with a host graph H. The Builder proposes a C4-free edge subset and
 the independent witness checker validates every edge is allowed and that no 4-cycle occurs. The obstruction
 channel constructs an upper certificate using exact optimization or a proved bound. BoundState is updated
 only after both receipts validate. If the gap remains wide, the representation worker may propose orbit
 partitions, codegree tables or PB encodings. Each transformation must preserve the original optimum under a
 checked bridge. A discovered general pattern is not promoted to theorem until formalized and checked in
 Lean or another accepted proof system.

 36.3 Required fixtures
   • K1-K6 and manually auditable small host graphs.
   • Positive fixtures containing known C4s and negative fixtures with none.
   • Adversarial edge-order/random-label permutations for semantic invariance.
   • Cases where universal complete-host bounds are invalid for arbitrary hosts, to prevent unsound transfer.
   • At least two independently implemented C4 predicates with differential property tests.

   • Return source plus compiler/checker receipts to the Coordinator.

   • Invoke a pinned Lean build in a clean environment.
   • Reject use of disallowed axioms or imports according to campaign policy.
   • Record the elaborated theorem identity and proof-check result.
   • Hash the complete Lean environment/toolchain metadata required for replay.
   • Distinguish elaboration error, timeout, kernel rejection and successful theorem check.

 41. Meta-gap discovery: how Genesis finds
 improvement #61
 Meta-gap discovery is the mechanism that converts architectural saturation into an empirical research
 problem. It does not start by inventing a fancy module name. It starts from repeated residual failure after the
 existing closure has been exercised.
      1. Collect frontier failures F that survive strong baselines and ordinary macro search.
      2. Cluster failures by invariant signature: what remains unsolved across representations/worlds?
      3. Enumerate/synthesize Research IR programs from current primitive basis K under cost bound B.
      4. If a program solves the cluster prospectively, no new primitive is needed; compile the program as a macro.
      5. If no adequate program exists, produce an expressivity/cost witness: current closure(K,B) misses capability C*.
      6. Synthesize candidate primitive p with explicit semantics, type signature, failure states and checker obligations.
      7. Add p only to sandboxed descendant K+p.
      8. Compare K and K+p on fresh tasks plus ablations and complexity accounting.
      9. Promote p to primitive only if it creates non-redundant prospective value; otherwise retain as macro or reject.
      10. Periodically retry deriving p from the enlarged basis; primitives may later be demoted.

 41.1 What counts as evidence that a primitive is genuinely new
   • A stable task/failure family lies outside the cost-bounded closure of current operators under the registered
      grammar/search budget.
   • The candidate primitive supplies a concise semantics, not merely a memorized task solution.
   • It transfers to fresh instances or a second family predicted by its semantics.
   • Ablating the primitive removes the gain or causes a clearly measured and reproducible compilation-cost
      explosion.
   • Its addition does not require weakening the evaluator or changing the target definition.


 42. Cognitive-kernel minimization experiment
 The initial 16 primitives are hypotheses. Genesis should experimentally search for a smaller basis while
 retaining verified reach.
   for primitive p in K:
       K_minus = K - {p}
       try:
            macro = synthesize_equivalent(p, grammar=K_minus, budget=B)
            measure = prospective_cost_and_capability(K_minus + {macro})
            if preservation_passes(measure) and cost_penalty <= threshold:
                demote p from primitive to macro
       except NoAdequateProgram:
            keep p

   Then search operator fusion/splitting candidates and rerun the same prospective test.

 This is not only software refactoring. If apparently different capabilities collapse to a common operator such
 as DISTINGUISH, QUOTIENT or TRANSPORT, Genesis has discovered a deeper structural regularity about its
 own problem-solving process.

 Appendix E. Glossary
  Term                                   Definition

  Artifact                               Immutable content-addressed object produced or consumed by a research action.

  Attempt                                One bounded execution of a tool/model/program; retries are separate attempts.

  Capability                             Typed operation with measured preconditions, postconditions, scope, cost and evidence.

  Certificate                            Replayable object sufficient for a registered checker to validate a claim under explicit assumptions.

  Checker                                Narrow program that validates a property; checker failure is distinct from claim failure.

  Claim                                  Human-facing proposition whose wording is gated by evidence status.

  Ghost                                  Validated countermodel satisfying visible assumptions and falsifying a target.

  Kernel                                 Either the immutable evidence microkernel or the evolvable cognitive primitive basis; context must
                                         disambiguate.

  Macro engine                           Reusable Research IR program compiled from primitives; not necessarily a permanent service.

  Phenotype                              Task-specific generated research machinery grown from the cognitive kernel and campaign context.

  Prospective                            Evaluation performed after candidate freeze on information not used to build/select the candidate.

  Research IR                            Typed executable intermediate representation for reasoning/research workflows.

  Sealed                                 Unavailable to proposal/search components until the preregistered evaluation phase.

  SystemVersion                          Complete immutable identity of all components that can affect comparison.

  World                                  A bounded alternative combination of representation, hypotheses, policies and capabilities maintained in the
                                         research ecology.

 Final statement
 Genesis should be judged by what it can independently establish, not by the sophistication of this
 specification. The architecture is intentionally ambitious, but its epistemic contract is conservative. The next
 decisive milestone is a clean, replayable prospective result. The final meta-capability is not another
 hand-designed engine; it is the ability to detect a reproducible failure outside the closure of the current
 cognitive basis, synthesize a genuinely new primitive or research program, and demonstrate on fresh
 evidence that the addition improves future verified discovery.

 If that happens repeatedly across generations and domains, the project will have evidence
 for the deepest Genesis claim. Until then, the correct description is: a comprehensive,
 implementable experimental architecture for proof-gated adaptive discovery and
 scientifically testable self-improvement.
